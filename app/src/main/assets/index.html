Keep Gmail quiet
Gmail controls its own alerts. Set this up once for your Instinct sender to avoid duplicate notifications.
  1. In Gmail on a computer, create a filter with From set to your Instinct email address.
  2. Select Skip the Inbox, apply a new Instinct label, and select Never mark as important. Mail stays in All Mail and in this app.
  3. In Gmail on your phone, open Settings → your account → Manage labels → Instinct and turn label notifications off. Check Important label alerts too. High priority notifications can override label settings.
Undo this anytime by deleting the filter in Gmail. This app cannot verify or change Gmail’s notification settings.
