Instinct for Android

Unofficial · Open source · MIT

A chat app for your Instinct emails. Read and reply through your Gmail account.

Download APKSetupGitHub

Requires Android 11+, a Gmail app password, and an Instinct email address. Text messages only; attachments open in Gmail. Checks for replies every 15 seconds while open and about every 15 minutes in the background.
