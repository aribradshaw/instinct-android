A few things to know.

This first version sends text. Attachments open in Gmail. Messages refresh every 15 seconds while the app is open; background checks run about every 15 minutes and may be delayed by Android battery management. “Sent” means Gmail accepted the email. It does not mean Instinct has read it.

Google app passwords require 2-Step Verification and are unavailable on some protected accounts. See the setup guide before installing.
