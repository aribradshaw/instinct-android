# Changelog

The public release history is powered by [@aribradshaw/devlog](https://github.com/aribradshaw/devlog) and published at [the Instinct Companion DevLog](https://aribradshaw.github.io/instinct-android/).

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-11

First public Android release: direct Gmail transport, chat-style conversation history, reply threading, encrypted local storage, saved drafts, uncertain-send tracking, periodic reply notifications, configurable account identities, and an in-app Public DevLog link.

Text messages only. Attachments open in Gmail. Background checks are periodic and subject to Android battery restrictions.
